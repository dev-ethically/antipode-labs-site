Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Antipode Labs Ltd (“we”, “us”) handles personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or use our apps, including Skin History.

Who we are

We are the data controller for personal information we collect through this website, our apps and by email. You can contact us at support@antipode-labs.com.

This website

This website is informational. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working. The hosting provider keeps these logs for a limited period and then deletes them.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you tell us. We use this only to reply to you and to handle your request. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. We keep emails only as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up, usually no longer than 12 months after your request is resolved, and then delete them.

Skin History app

Skin History is designed to keep your information on your own device.

  • What the app stores: the photos, notes and other entries you add to the app. These are stored on your device.
  • Camera and photos: the app asks your permission to use the camera, to choose photos from your photo library, and to save photos to an album in your Photos library. It uses these only so you can add photos to your entries and keep them together. You can change these permissions at any time in your device’s Settings.
  • What we collect: we do not collect, receive or have access to your photos, notes or entries, and we do not require an account.
  • Analytics and advertising: the app does not include advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking that identifies you. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which do not identify you.
  • No sharing or selling: because we do not receive your data, we do not sell it or share it with third parties.
  • Purchases: if you buy the app or make an in-app purchase, payment is handled by Apple. We do not receive your name or payment details.
  • Apple services: if you use iCloud Backup, your device backup, including the app’s data, is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. If you use iCloud Photos, photos the app saves to your Photos library are also stored there. This is controlled by your device settings and Apple’s privacy policy, not by us.
  • Deleting your data: you can delete entries inside the app, or delete the app to remove its data from your device. Deleting the app does not remove photos it saved to your Photos library, or copies held in iCloud backups; you can manage those in the Photos app and your Apple Account settings.

Skin History records information about your skin, which is health-related. Please keep your device secure, for example with a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. The app is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice or diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or other health professional. If you are worried about a change in your skin, speak to a qualified health professional.

Children

Our website and apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or give you a copy of it. To use these rights, email support@antipode-labs.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Service providers

We use a website hosting provider and an email provider. They process personal information on our behalf, under contract, and only to provide their services to us. We do not sell personal information.

International transfers

We do not transfer personal information we hold outside the UK ourselves. Our email and website hosting providers may process data in other countries under appropriate safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we release a new app or change how an app works. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. We will publish the updated version on this page.
